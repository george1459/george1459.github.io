About

Hi there! I am a final-year CS Ph.D. candidate at Stanford, advised by Prof. Monica S. Lam at the Stanford NLP Group. My research focuses on knowledge agents with LLMs, aiming to enable domain-independent approaches for in-depth exploration and synthesis of information from diverse knowledge sources — structured, unstructured, and hybrid. Some of my recent work includes reinforcement learning approaches (SCRIBES, ICLR 2026; work done at Meta), agentic frameworks (DataSTORM, COLM 2026; SPINACH, EMNLP 2024), and programming-language interfaces for LLMs (SUQL, NAACL 2024) to tackle this problem.

Over the summer of 2026, I am spending time at Databricks, bridging a research collaboration with Stanford. Previously, I interned at Meta Reality Labs and Meta FAIR.

I am grateful to be supported by the 2025–2026 IBM PhD Fellowship Award.
